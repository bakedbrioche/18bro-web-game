# Authored 18Bro, rig version 5

`18bro.blend` is generated by `scripts/blender/create_18bro.py` in Blender 5.2.2 LTS.
The self-contained runtime file is `dist/assets/models/18bro-v1.glb`; the local
`manifest.json` records the current hash, bounds, budgets, and clip contract.
The chief integrates this entry into the shared manifests after validation.

The armature has 20 bones: the existing names and five sockets, plus paired
forearms, shins, and feet. The head and torso share one sculpted surface. Limb
caps enter the shoulder and hip surfaces without dragging those surfaces when
the joints bend. Geometry remains under 4,000 triangles, with three materials,
three draws, an embedded 512 x 512 palette, and at most two weights per vertex.

Every clip contains complete quaternion, translation, and scale tracks for all
14 moving bones. Sampling is 48 fps, with linear interpolation and identical
first/last loop poses. The static simulation root never moves. Only the imported
animation mixer may write these bones; do not multiply Original poses into them.
Action keys and NLA strips begin at Blender frame 0. Export validation checks
every actual GLB sampler starts at time 0 and ends at the declared duration,
with all 60 transform channels, normalized quaternions, and closed loop seams.

Run contact starts with the left paw forward and the right paw behind. Each paw
has a stance for 46% of its cycle, followed by a smooth recovery, offset by half
a cycle from the other paw. In-place stance moves from model Y = -0.18 to +0.18
while the ankle stays at Z = 0.112. Swing lifts 0.19 above that plane. Baked
two-bone IK and counter-rotating foot bones maintain the contacts; no runtime IK
or constraint evaluation is needed. Walk uses a 62% stance and smaller stride.

All sockets have an upright model-space rest basis: Blender bone +Y maps to
model +Z; after glTF conversion their world basis is ordinary Y-up with +Z
forward. Use socket world position, quaternion, and scale, or parent directly
to the socket. `socket_hat` is centered at Blender Z = 2.30. Carry sockets are
children of the forearms at the wrist centers. The back socket remains on the
spine. Hat and fur material names remain `hat` and `fur`.

To regenerate source, runtime GLB, local manifest, validation, and review PNGs:

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe' --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 --python 'scripts/blender/create_18bro.py'
```

Append `-- --skip-render` for geometry/animation validation alone. Review output
is deliberately under untracked `release/model-review/`. Inspect the front,
rear, side, run contact sheet, and action contact sheet. Their ordering is in
`contact-sheet-order.json`. The builder checks every frame for finite bounded
skin, planted contact accuracy, loop closure, root motion, weight normalization,
and size/triangle budgets. Real gameplay validation is still required because
the game owns root placement, blend timing, prop offsets, and capture staging.

The mascot retains its sleepy static facial expression; facial shape keys and
finger articulation are outside this rig. These are deliberate compact game
poses, with the readable rear silhouette taking priority over invisible detail.
