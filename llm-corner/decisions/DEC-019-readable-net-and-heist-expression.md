# DEC-019 - Present one readable pursuit net and deform the existing heist face

Date: 2026-09-28
Status: ACCEPTED
Supersedes: DEC-018's always-visible escort net and runtime face-overlay proposal

## Context

Two full-size net props filled the rear camera during every pursuit stage. Their overlapping hoops hid 18Bro and the telegraph, while radial open-web lines read as wheels. The opening also kept the normal sleepy face after the packet was stolen. A first correction added duplicate panic eyes over the model, which produced broken intersections and violated the requirement to animate the existing face. The packet was assembled from overlapping primitives and did not read as one sealed snack bag.

## Decision

Only the guard taking the current attempt presents a net. The open state uses sagging horizontal and vertical chords inside a sixteen-segment hoop. The caught state uses one tapered connected sack, a fixed payload socket and the existing world-down basis. The selected guard's real hand remains the only prop owner. During carry, the rigid shaft is derived through the selected shoulder anchor; the tested centreline-to-shoulder distance is effectively zero. The escort keeps no visible second hoop, both officers share one exit pace, and the group hides after the bounded exit.

The opening deforms existing character geometry only. Original stores and restores the transforms of its existing eyes, pupils, eyelids, brows and mouth. Refreshed clones its existing face and fur geometries once, identifies the two disconnected 62-vertex eyelid components, lifts and compresses them while reshaping the existing palette-mapped eyes, pupils, brows and mouth, then restores the immutable position arrays exactly. No panic-eye mesh is added to either model family.

The stolen packet is one indexed sealed-pillow mesh with rounded depth and integrated crimp bands. Two modeled CHIPS panels sit on its surfaces. Original and Refreshed clone the same scene-authored prop. The cap covers the prior diamond with a small geometry-only orange/red/green seven-inspired mark; its color layers are offset beyond the beveled backing so they remain visible at gameplay scale.

## Consequences

Wind-up, strike, enclosure and shoulder carry each have one readable focal prop. Tests protect fixed scale, payload containment, hand ownership, shoulder contact, bounded exit and exact expression restoration. The packet and cap remain local scene geometry and are included automatically in modular and standalone builds.
