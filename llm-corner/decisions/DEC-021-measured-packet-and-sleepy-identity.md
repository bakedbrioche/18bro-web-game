# DEC-021 - Measure packet volume and preserve 18Bro's official sleepy identity

Date: 2026-09-28
Status: ACCEPTED
Amends: DEC-019's packet, surprise-face and cap-mark details; DEC-020's peeking favicon details

## Context

The first sealed packet still depended on front/back presentation and exposed a flat or broken silhouette when the wrist turned. The Refreshed surprise enlarged existing eye regions but left their surface behind the fur eyelids, clipping the lower eye corners. The cap used floating colored blocks that intersected at gameplay scale. The first peeking favicon carried the surprised expression rather than 18Bro's recognizable sleepy brows and half-lidded eyes.

## Decision

Construct the packet as one closed indexed analytic pillow. A 16 × 16 grid per face plus connected top, bottom and side boundaries produces 1,152 triangles; every unique edge must have incidence two. Depth tapers only into the integrated heat seals and side gussets. Red artwork is a body material zone, and both CHIPS wordmarks bend to remain 0.0035 units above the same analytic surface instead of floating as planes.

Animate surprise with existing geometry only. Original moves its eye whites and pupils forward while lifting and retracting its eyelids; Refreshed moves its existing eye/pupil vertices forward by measured local depth while retracting the two existing fur-eyelid components. Both families restore their exact starting data when the beat ends.

Build the cap badge as shallow ordered geometry on the panel surface: white badge, orange bar, one curved red 7, and green block-built ELEVEN. Use the supplied official front view as the identity anchor for the browser favicon: calm low brows, half-lidded oval eyes, forehead, ears and branded cap on orange. Archive the preceding peeking icon and retain the selected full-resolution source and prompt in the ignored dated image workbench.

## Consequences

Geometry tests now protect packet manifold closure, dimensions, gusset taper, artwork orientation and surface conformity; expression tests protect eye-to-lid depth clearance and exact restoration; badge tests protect layer ordering, bounds and gameplay-scale word width. The standalone exporter continues to embed the exact active favicon and all runtime geometry remains local and offline.
